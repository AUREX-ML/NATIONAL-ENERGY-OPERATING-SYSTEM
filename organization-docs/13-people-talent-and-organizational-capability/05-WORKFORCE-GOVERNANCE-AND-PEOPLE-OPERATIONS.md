# Workforce Governance & People Operations

## Purpose

Turn the people architecture into a controlled workforce operating system.

```text
Strategy
  ↓
Workforce Plan
  ↓
Role & Headcount Governance
  ↓
Employment Lifecycle
  ↓
People Operations
  ↓
Performance & Development
  ↓
Succession
  ↓
Institutional Capability
```

## Workforce Planning

Plan capability and capacity from strategic objectives, roadmap, operating demand and financial constraints. For each capability, identify current coverage, future need, gap, acquisition method and timing.

## Headcount Governance

Every permanent role creates recurring financial obligations. A new position therefore requires a capability justification, role definition, manager, cost, budget, start timing and expected outcome.

```text
Capability Gap → Role Request → Management Review → Financial Review → Approval → Recruitment
```

## Position Governance

Maintain the distinction between organizational positions and current occupants. At scale, maintain a position registry with position ID, role, function, level, manager, location, employment type, budget status and occupancy status.

## Employment Lifecycle

```text
Need → Role → Recruit → Select → Contract → Onboard → Perform → Develop → Reward → Transition → Offboard
```

Maintain legitimate personnel records with appropriate privacy, role-based access and data minimization.

## Policy Architecture

```text
Governance Principles → People Policies → Procedures → Manager Guidance → Work Instructions
```

Policies should address real recurring decisions, legal obligations or material risks. Each controlled policy needs an owner, approver, version, effective date, review date and change history.

## Compensation & Payroll

Maintain controlled records for compensation, benefits, allowances, variable compensation and equity where applicable. Compensation changes require defined authorization.

Payroll should follow authorized workforce data, calculation, review, payment, statutory obligations and reconciliation, with segregation of duties developing as scale increases.

## Performance & Learning Records

Material performance decisions should preserve expectations, observed performance, feedback/support and resulting decisions. Track operationally significant training, especially safety, cybersecurity, compliance, field operations, control systems and incident response.

## Skills & Key-Person Risk

Maintain visibility into critical skills and system knowledge. Where one person's departure could remove a critical capability, mitigate through documentation, pairing, training, automation, secondary ownership and succession.

## Joiner–Mover–Leaver Controls

```text
JOINER → provision appropriate access
MOVER  → review and change access
LEAVER → revoke access and recover assets
```

Privileged production, security, financial, control and personnel-data access requires stronger governance.

## Offboarding

Cover notice, handover, knowledge transfer, customer/partner transfer, asset return, access revocation, financial settlement, record updates and exit feedback. Security-sensitive departures may require coordinated immediate access removal.

## People Analytics

Useful measures may include headcount, workforce cost, time to hire, time to productivity, quality of hire, voluntary/regrettable attrition, internal mobility, promotion, succession coverage, training completion, absence and on-call load.

Analytics exists to improve decisions, not maximize employee surveillance.

## Integration

Workforce planning connects directly to:

- Strategy → required capability
- Product roadmap → engineering capability
- Deployment roadmap → operational capacity
- Financial model → affordability, burn and runway
- Operating model → roles and service requirements
- Governance → workforce authority
- Risk → people controls and key-person dependencies
- Open source → ecosystem capability

## Stage Model

**Founder-led prototype:** role documentation, contractor agreements, access control, source-control governance, knowledge documentation and capability map.

**Pilot:** workforce plan, formal employment documentation, onboarding, performance expectations, leave/payroll, security training and core policies.

**Commercial:** job levels, compensation structure, management systems, analytics, performance cycles, learning programs, succession and handbook.

**Infrastructure institution:** formal people governance, regional workforce planning, leadership pipelines, 24/7 operations workforce, deep succession and specialist compliance.

## Workforce Governance Laws

1. Workforce planning derives from strategy, operating demand and required capability.
2. Headcount is a recurring capital commitment requiring economic and organizational justification.
3. Positions belong to the organization; people temporarily occupy them.
4. Capability and capacity are measured separately.
5. Workforce information is collected only for legitimate purposes and protected according to sensitivity.
6. Compensation, performance, promotion and potential are related but distinct decisions.
7. Workforce events trigger review of authority, access and institutional ownership.
8. Critical workforce dependencies appear in enterprise risk management.
9. Every critical capability progressively gains documentation, redundancy and succession.
10. Founder-held knowledge and authority progressively become institutional knowledge and governed authority.
11. People analytics exists to improve organizational decisions rather than maximize surveillance.
12. Workforce, product, operating and financial planning operate as one interconnected system.

## Definition of Done

The domain is operational when the institution can determine required capabilities and capacity; govern positions and headcount; administer the workforce lifecycle; protect personnel information; connect workforce changes to access; operate performance, compensation and policy systems; identify key-person risks; maintain succession; measure workforce health; and preserve institutional capability through organizational growth and personnel change.
