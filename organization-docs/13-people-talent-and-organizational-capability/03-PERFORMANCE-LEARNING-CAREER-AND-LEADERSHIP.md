# Performance, Learning, Career & Leadership

## Purpose

Convert individual capability into sustained performance, deeper expertise, leadership capacity and institutional continuity.

## Performance System

```text
Mission → Objectives → Role Outcomes → Execution → Evidence → Feedback → Development
```

Performance should consider outcomes, quality, reliability, collaboration, learning, safety/compliance and behavior relevant to the role.

## Performance Cycle

1. Establish objectives and expectations.
2. Maintain ongoing feedback.
3. Review progress during the cycle.
4. Conduct evidence-based performance review.
5. Identify development priorities.
6. Make compensation/promotion decisions through appropriate governance.
7. Establish the next objectives.

Performance, promotion, compensation and potential are related but distinct decisions.

## Learning Architecture

```text
Capability Need → Learning Goal → Practice → Feedback → Demonstrated Competence → Knowledge Transfer
```

Use project work, mentoring, technical reviews, documentation, open-source contribution, structured training, incident learning and formal education where useful.

## Career Architecture

Maintain both technical and management paths.

```text
Technical: Engineer → Senior → Staff → Principal / Distinguished
Management: Team Lead → Manager → Director → Executive
```

Exact titles may evolve, but management should never be the only route to advancement.

## Leadership

Leadership capability includes judgment, communication, delegation, decision quality, people development, accountability, systems thinking, conflict handling and succession building.

Managers are accountable not only for output but also for the capability and health of their teams.

## Promotion

Promotion should reflect sustained evidence that the individual is already operating at or near the scope, complexity, autonomy and impact expected at the next level.

Promotion is not simply a reward for tenure.

## Succession

Critical leadership and technical roles require:

- Identified backups
- Potential successors
- Readiness assessment
- Development actions
- Knowledge transfer
- Emergency authority continuity

## Definition of Done

The organization can define good performance, provide useful feedback, develop capability deliberately, offer credible technical and management careers, promote from evidence, develop managers, and maintain succession for critical leadership and technical roles.
