# 13 — People, Talent & Organizational Capability

This module defines how the organization builds and governs the human capability required to execute its mission—from workforce design and hiring through performance, culture, people operations, succession, and institutional continuity.

> **Master People & Organizational Capability Law:** The organization shall treat human capability as institutional infrastructure: planning it from strategy, acquiring it deliberately, developing it continuously, governing authority and employment responsibly, protecting people and institutional knowledge throughout the workforce lifecycle, and systematically converting individual competence into teams, systems, leadership, successors and ecosystem capability capable of carrying the mission beyond any individual person.

```text
Institutional Capability
```

## Core Commitments

- Capability before headcount.
- Roles before titles.
- Accountability before authority expansion.
- Evidence before promotion.
- Sustainable workforce economics before payroll expansion.
- Technical and management careers remain distinct.
- Documentation and teaching convert individual expertise into institutional knowledge.
- Founder dependency and all other key-person dependencies are progressively reduced.
- Open-source communities, universities, partners, contractors and employees form a wider capability ecosystem.
- Workforce planning remains connected to strategy, roadmap, operations, finance and risk.
