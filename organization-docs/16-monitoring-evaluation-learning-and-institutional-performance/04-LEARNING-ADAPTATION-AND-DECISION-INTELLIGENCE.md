# Learning, Adaptation & Decision Intelligence

## Purpose

Institutional learning converts evidence and experience into durable improvements to products, architecture, operations, governance and strategy.

> Evidence has limited institutional value if it never changes what the organization decides, builds, funds, standardizes or stops.

## Learning Loop

```text
Observe
  ↓
Interpret
  ↓
Hypothesize
  ↓
Test
  ↓
Learn
  ↓
Decide
  ↓
Change
  ↓
Measure Again
```

## Institutional Learning

A person learning is not the same as an institution learning. Institutional learning requires experience to be captured, validated, converted into knowledge, reflected in decisions and processes, transferred to others and retained through personnel transitions.

## Learning Orders

- Single-loop: improve execution of the current approach.
- Double-loop: question whether the underlying approach is correct.
- Higher-order learning: improve how the institution itself learns and decides.

## Hypothesis Governance

Major assumptions should become explicit hypotheses where feasible. A Hypothesis Register should capture the assumption, consequence, uncertainty, test, required evidence, owner, status and resulting decision.

High-uncertainty/high-consequence assumptions should be tested early.

## Experiment Architecture

```text
Question
  ↓
Hypothesis
  ↓
Test
  ↓
Success / Failure Criteria
  ↓
Data
  ↓
Result
  ↓
Decision
```

Build the smallest safe experiment capable of reducing the important uncertainty.

## Decision Intelligence

```text
Question + Data + Context + Models + Experience + Risk + Objectives
                              ↓
                       Decision Support
```

Decision intelligence supports legitimate authority; it does not replace it.

## Decision Classes

Decision process should scale with consequence and reversibility:

- Type 1: high consequence / difficult to reverse
- Type 2: moderate consequence / reversible
- Type 3: routine operational decision

## Decision Register

Material decisions should preserve:

- Question
- Options
- Evidence
- Assumptions
- Risks
- Decision
- Owner
- Date
- Expected result
- Review date

Decision quality should later be assessed separately from outcome luck.

## Lessons

A useful lesson states what happened, why, the consequence, what should change and who owns the change.

```text
Observation
  ↓
Proposed Lesson
  ↓
Validation
  ↓
Accepted Lesson
  ↓
Action
  ↓
Standard Change
  ↓
Verification
```

Lessons that change nothing are not yet institutionalized.

## Incident Learning

```text
Incident
  ↓
Contain
  ↓
Recover
  ↓
Root Cause
  ↓
Lesson
  ↓
Corrective / Preventive Action
  ↓
Standard Change
  ↓
Retest
```

Root-cause analysis should examine system conditions rather than stop at individual error.

## Learning Propagation

```text
Facility Lesson → Validation → Relevant Facilities / Regions → Network Standard
```

Learning should also flow downward from national insight to regional and facility practice.

## Knowledge Governance

Critical knowledge should be captured through documentation, decision records, runbooks, architecture records, standards, procedures and training. Tacit knowledge may require pairing, shadowing, mentoring, simulation and role rotation.

Knowledge has a lifecycle:

```text
Create → Review → Approve → Use → Update → Supersede → Archive
```

## Adaptive Strategy

```text
Mission  → stable
Strategy → adaptive
Tactics  → highly adaptive
```

Technology, architecture and business models are methods, not sacred objects. Strong evidence may require resource reallocation, strategic pivots or abandonment of an initiative.

## Principles

1. Evidence must be capable of changing decisions.
2. Critical assumptions are explicit and testable.
3. Experiments exist to reduce uncertainty.
4. Decision process is proportional to consequence.
5. Material decisions preserve evidence and rationale.
6. Lessons produce owned changes.
7. Failures and disconfirming evidence remain institutional memory.
8. Successful patterns are validated before standardization.
9. Critical knowledge migrates from individuals into durable systems.
10. Adaptations are measured again to verify improvement.

## Definition of Done

Learning is mature when major assumptions are visible, important uncertainty is deliberately tested, material decisions are traceable, lessons and incidents produce verified changes, learning propagates across relevant facilities and regions, critical knowledge survives personnel transitions, strategy can adapt to evidence, and resulting adaptations are measured again.
