# Compensation & Rewards

## Purpose

Compensation supports attraction, retention, fairness, accountability, and sustainable execution of the mission.

## Principles

Compensation decisions should consider:

- role scope and responsibility;
- required competence;
- contribution and performance;
- relevant market evidence;
- internal consistency;
- scarcity of capability;
- organizational financial capacity;
- applicable legal requirements.

## Architecture

```text
Role Architecture
  ↓
Level / Responsibility
  ↓
Compensation Range
  ↓
Individual Placement
  ↓
Performance / Contribution
  ↓
Review
```

## Components

Where appropriate, total rewards may include base compensation, variable compensation, benefits, equity or long-term incentives, development opportunities, and non-financial recognition.

## Controls

Material compensation changes require authorized approval and a traceable record. Individuals should not possess unrestricted authority to approve their own compensation, bonus, equity, or equivalent benefits.

## Sustainability Law

Compensation architecture must remain compatible with the organization's financial model, cash flow, runway, and long-term obligations.
