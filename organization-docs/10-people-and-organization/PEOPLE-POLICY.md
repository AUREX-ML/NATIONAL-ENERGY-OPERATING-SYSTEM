# People Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes the baseline principles governing employment, workforce decisions, authority, conduct, development, compensation, records, and organizational continuity.

## Policy Principles

1. Workforce decisions shall support legitimate organizational capability requirements.
2. Employment decisions shall use relevant evidence and fair process.
3. Authority shall be proportionate to role, competence, responsibility, and risk.
4. Safety, law, ethics, and professional obligations take precedence over commercial pressure.
5. Compensation and workforce expansion shall remain compatible with financial sustainability.
6. Employees shall be treated professionally and may raise legitimate concerns without inappropriate retaliation.
7. Material workforce transactions require appropriate authorization and records.
8. Personal information shall be collected, accessed, retained, and deleted for legitimate purposes and in accordance with applicable requirements.
9. Critical roles shall receive continuity treatment through documentation, redundancy, cross-training, or succession.
10. The organization shall progressively reduce uncontrolled dependency on individual people.

## Policy Governance

Every material People policy should identify its owner, approver, scope, version, effective date, and review date.

Policies are supported by executable processes, controls, evidence, and controlled exception procedures.

## Local Requirements

Core institutional principles remain consistent while employment implementation must comply with applicable requirements in each jurisdiction where the organization operates.
