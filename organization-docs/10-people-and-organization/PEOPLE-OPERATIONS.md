# People Operations

## Purpose

People Operations makes organizational rules executable throughout the workforce lifecycle.

## Lifecycle

```text
Candidate
  ↓
Hire
  ↓
Onboard
  ↓
Authorize
  ↓
Perform & Develop
  ↓
Move
  ↓
Offboard
  ↓
Retain / Dispose Records
```

## System of Record

The organization should maintain an authoritative source for appropriate workforce information including identity, employment status, role, manager, location, compensation, contract, training, leave, and employment history.

## Joiner Control

A new hire triggers coordinated creation of the people record, payroll setup, organizational identity, equipment, required training, system access, role authorization, and management ownership.

## Mover Control

A role change triggers authorized updates to role, manager, objectives, compensation where applicable, decision rights, and system access. Obsolete authority must be removed as deliberately as new authority is granted.

## Leaver Control

At the appropriate effective time, departure triggers access revocation, authority removal, asset recovery, payroll closure, knowledge transfer, responsibility reassignment, and controlled record retention.

## Payroll Control

```text
Valid Person?
  ↓
Active Status?
  ↓
Authorized Compensation?
  ↓
Approved Change?
  ↓
Pay
```

Payroll should be periodically reconciled against authoritative workforce and financial records.

## Data Governance

Collect only workforce information required for legitimate purposes. Apply appropriate least-privilege access, audit trails, retention periods, archival rules, and secure deletion.

## Automation

High-value automation includes onboarding tasks, role-change access reviews, termination access revocation, training expiry alerts, probation reviews, and contract-expiry workflows. Automation supports workflow and control enforcement but does not replace legitimate human judgment in consequential employment decisions.
