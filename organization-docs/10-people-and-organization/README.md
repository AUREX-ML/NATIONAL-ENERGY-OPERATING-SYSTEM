# 10 — People & Organization

The **People & Organization** module defines how the organization converts mission and strategy into human capability through organizational design, roles, hiring, performance, culture, compensation, succession, ethics, people operations, and governance.

> **Master People Law:** Build people into an institution—not around an individual: define capability before headcount, roles before titles, accountability before authority, competence before control, evidence before promotion, sustainability before payroll expansion, and succession before dependency becomes failure.

## Core Principles

1. Organizational structure follows required capability, not prestige.
2. Roles exist independently of the people occupying them.
3. Every material outcome has identifiable accountability.
4. Hiring is controlled acquisition of capability.
5. Employment does not automatically grant unrestricted authority.
6. Performance is evaluated through outcomes, quality, behavior, and responsibility.
7. Compensation must remain compatible with institutional sustainability.
8. Culture is defined through repeated behavior, leadership example, incentives, and tolerated conduct.
9. Reality and evidence outrank hierarchy, confidence, and ambition.
10. Critical knowledge, access, and authority progressively move from individual dependency into institutional systems.
11. Workforce planning connects strategy, workload, risk, and financial capacity.
12. No individual—including the founder—should ultimately remain an uncontrolled single point of institutional failure.

## Relationship to Other Modules

The **Operating Model** defines how work moves through the institution.

The **Financial Model** defines the economic capacity supporting the workforce.

The **Risk Management** module defines the controls governing people-related exposure, authority, safety, security, and continuity.

The **People & Organization** module defines the human capability required to execute all three.
