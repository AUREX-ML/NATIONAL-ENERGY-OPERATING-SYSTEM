# Role Architecture

## Purpose

Role architecture separates institutional responsibilities from the individual currently performing them.

## Role Definition

Every material role should define:

- purpose;
- accountable outcomes;
- responsibilities;
- decision rights;
- authority limits;
- required competence;
- interfaces and dependencies;
- escalation responsibilities;
- continuity requirements.

## Role vs Person

```text
ROLE
  ↓
Purpose + Accountability + Authority + Competence
  ↓
POSITION
  ↓
PERSON
```

A person may temporarily occupy several roles in an early-stage organization. Those roles should still be documented separately so they can later be delegated without redesigning the institution from memory.

## Critical Authority

Employment alone does not authorize infrastructure control, production administration, financial approval, security administration, or other high-consequence actions. Authority must follow legitimate role requirements and demonstrated competence.

## Role Changes

A role change must review both new and obsolete authority. Granting new permissions without removing old permissions creates accumulated access and control risk.
