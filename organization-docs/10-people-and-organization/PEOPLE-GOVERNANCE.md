# People Governance

## Purpose

People Governance defines who may make workforce decisions, under what rules, using what evidence, within what boundaries, and with what accountability.

## Governance Stack

```text
Governance
  ↓
Policies
  ↓
Processes
  ↓
Controls
  ↓
Records & Evidence
```

## Responsibilities

### Governance / Board

As maturity requires, governance oversees executive appointment and succession, executive compensation, major restructuring, material workforce risk, serious executive misconduct, and institutional culture and ethics oversight.

### CEO

The CEO is accountable for organizational capability, leadership quality, culture, workforce sustainability, the executive team, and succession.

### People Function

The People function owns architecture, policy, process, workforce data, advice, assurance, and capability-development infrastructure. It does not replace managers.

### Managers

Managers own work, expectations, feedback, performance, development, and team health within legitimate authority.

### Employees

Employees are accountable for legitimate role responsibilities, competence, professional conduct, organizational resources, disclosure of material conflicts, and escalation of material concerns.

## People Risk

Material People risks include key-person dependency, skills shortage, turnover, leadership failure, misconduct, fraud, workforce fatigue, unauthorized access, and succession failure.

## Control Model

Each important control should define:

```text
Control → Owner → Frequency → Evidence → Exception Process
```

## Workforce Dashboard

Leadership reporting may include headcount, workforce cost, capability gaps, hiring, turnover, critical-role coverage, succession, performance, training compliance, culture indicators, and material People risks.

## Governance Principle

Founder authority should progressively be converted into defined roles, processes, delegated authority, independent controls, and institutional governance as organizational consequence grows.
