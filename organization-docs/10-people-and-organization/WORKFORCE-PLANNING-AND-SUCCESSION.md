# Workforce Planning & Succession

## Purpose

Workforce planning converts future strategy and workload into an executable capability plan. Succession protects continuity when people move, leave, or become unavailable.

## Workforce Planning

```text
Strategy + Roadmap
       ↓
Future Work
       ↓
Required Capabilities
       ↓
Current Capacity
       ↓
Capability Gap
       ↓
Develop / Automate / Contract / Hire
```

Planning should consider workload, criticality, geography, operating hours, financial capacity, and expected business maturity.

## Critical Roles

A role is critical when its absence could materially disrupt safety, infrastructure control, service continuity, revenue, regulatory obligations, security, or strategic execution.

## Succession Treatment

Critical roles should progressively receive one or more of:

- documented procedures;
- cross-training;
- deputy or alternate coverage;
- knowledge transfer;
- access continuity planning;
- internal successor development;
- external succession options.

## Key-Person Risk

```text
Critical Capability
  ↓
One Person Only?
  ↓ YES
Continuity Risk
  ↓
Document + Cross-train + Delegate + Succession
```

## Continuity Law

No individual—including the founder—should ultimately remain an uncontrolled single point of institutional failure.
