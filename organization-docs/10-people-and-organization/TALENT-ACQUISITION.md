# Talent Acquisition & Onboarding

## Principle

Hiring is the controlled acquisition of capability.

## Hiring Trigger

```text
Required Outcome
  ↓
Capability Requirement
  ↓
Gap Analysis
  ↓
Build / Automate / Contract / Hire
  ↓
Approved Position
```

A vacancy alone is not sufficient justification for hiring.

## Selection

Selection should evaluate evidence relevant to the role, including competence, problem-solving ability, professional conduct, learning capacity, and mission-relevant experience.

High-consequence roles may require appropriate credential, reference, or background verification where justified and lawful.

## Offer and Hiring Controls

Before employment begins, establish approved role, employment terms, compensation, required verification, contract, payroll setup, equipment needs, identity preparation, and onboarding responsibilities.

## Onboarding

```text
Hire
  ↓
People Record
  ↓
Identity
  ↓
Equipment
  ↓
Training
  ↓
Role Context
  ↓
Authorized Access
  ↓
Objectives
```

Onboarding is complete when the person can safely and effectively execute the intended role—not merely when paperwork is signed.
