# People & Organization Framework

## Purpose

This framework defines how the organization develops and governs the human capability required to execute its mission across research, prototype, facility EMS, regional DERMS, and national DERMS maturity.

## System Objective

The People system exists not merely to administer employees, but to continuously create the capability required to execute the organization's mission safely, ethically, sustainably, and at institutional scale.

## Architecture

```text
Mission
  ↓
People Strategy
  ↓

  ↓
Roles & Authority
  ↓
Talent Acquisition
  ↓

Culture & Rewards

  ↓
People Operations
  ↓
Governance & Assurance
  ↓
Organizational Capability
```

## Capability Before Headcount

Organizational design begins by asking what capabilities the mission requires. Headcount is a consequence of capability requirements, workload, risk, and financial capacity—not a measure of institutional success.

## Institutionalization

The organization should mature through four states:

```text
Founder = Organization
        ↓
Founder + People = Team
        ↓
Roles + Processes + People = Organization
        ↓
Governance + Capability + Culture + Systems + Succession = Institution
```

Founder authority should progressively become explicit roles, delegated decision rights, documented processes, controls, and governance.

## Integration

People governance connects directly with:

- strategy and roadmap planning;
- financial planning and workforce cost;
- cybersecurity identity and access management;
- safety competency and authorization;
- operational staffing and coverage;
- enterprise risk management;
- business expansion and regional capability;
- governance and succession.

## Governing Laws

- Structure exists to provide mission-required capability.
- Roles define purpose, authority, accountability, boundaries, and competence.
- Accountability precedes authority.
- Competence precedes critical control authority.
- Evidence outranks hierarchy.
- Critical knowledge must become institutional knowledge.
- Managers are accountable for outcomes and capability development.
- Workforce expansion must remain economically sustainable.
- Succession is a continuity control.
- No individual should remain an uncontrolled institutional dependency.
