# Performance Management

## Purpose

Performance management aligns individual contribution with organizational outcomes while developing stronger capability over time.

## Performance Model

```text
Mission
  ↓
Strategy
  ↓
Team Outcomes
  ↓
Role Outcomes
  ↓
Objectives
  ↓
Execution
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Feedback + Development
```

## Evaluation Dimensions

Performance should consider:

- outcomes delivered;
- quality and reliability;
- professional behavior;
- accountability;
- collaboration;
- learning and capability growth;
- adherence to safety, security, ethics, and legitimate controls.

Activity alone is not performance.

## Management Responsibilities

Managers establish expectations, provide timely feedback, remove legitimate blockers, document material performance issues, support development, and make evidence-based recommendations.

## Underperformance

Where appropriate:

```text
Expectation
  ↓
Feedback
  ↓
Support / Improvement Plan
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Decision
```

Consequential employment decisions require legitimate judgment and fair process rather than automatic scoring rules.
