# Culture & Values

## Principle

Culture is the repeated behavior the organization rewards, tolerates, corrects, and models through leadership.

## Operating Values

The organization should reinforce:

- evidence over narrative;
- mission over status;
- accountability with authority;
- open technical challenge;
- safety and professional responsibility;
- collaboration across disciplines;
- continuous learning;
- stewardship of infrastructure and public value;
- open standards over unnecessary proprietary dependence.

## Evidence Culture

> Nobody may manufacture evidence to make reality conform to an organizational narrative.

Technical evidence, operational reality, safety concerns, and material risk must be capable of challenging hierarchy.

## Healthy Disagreement

Challenge ideas, designs, assumptions, and decisions rigorously. Do not normalize humiliation, intimidation, harassment, retaliation, or personal abuse.

## Leadership Example

Leadership behavior carries greater cultural weight than written values. Incentives, promotions, resource allocation, and responses to failure should reinforce the behaviors the institution claims to value.

## Learning

Good-faith failures should be examined for lessons and systemic improvement. Deliberate falsification, concealment, reckless conduct, or repeated disregard for legitimate controls require different treatment.
