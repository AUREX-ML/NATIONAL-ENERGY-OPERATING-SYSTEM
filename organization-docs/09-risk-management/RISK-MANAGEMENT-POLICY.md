# Risk Management Policy

## Policy Statement

The organization will deliberately pursue innovation and infrastructure transformation while maintaining material risks within authorized boundaries.

Risk management shall be integrated into strategy, investment, engineering, product development, deployment, operations, finance, commercial decisions, cybersecurity, safety, regulatory compliance, partnerships, and governance.

## Mandatory Requirements

1. Material risks shall be identified and documented.
2. Every material risk shall have one accountable owner.
3. Risks shall be assessed using an approved methodology.
4. Inherent and residual exposure shall be distinguished.
5. Residual risk shall be compared with approved appetite and tolerance.
6. Risks outside tolerance shall have documented treatment and escalation.
7. Material controls shall have identifiable owners and evidence.
8. High-consequence risks shall use proportionate defense in depth.
9. Critical incidents shall be escalated without unnecessary delay.
10. Material incidents, near misses, findings, and control failures shall feed organizational learning.
11. Risk assessments shall be refreshed after material changes in scale, architecture, authority, regulation, customers, financing, or operating environment.
12. Human safety shall take precedence over optimization, availability, revenue, and schedule.
13. The organization shall not exercise infrastructure control beyond its technical, contractual, professional, and legal authority.
14. No individual may unilaterally override established hard safety, legal, anti-fraud, or ethical boundaries.

## Risk Ownership

Operating functions own the risks arising from their activities. Risk and compliance functions establish methodology, monitor aggregation, challenge management, and coordinate enterprise reporting. Independent assurance provides proportionate third-line assessment.

## Risk Acceptance

Risk may only be accepted by an authority appropriate to its residual severity. Acceptance must be documented and time-bounded where conditions may materially change.

Hard-limit conditions are not eligible for discretionary acceptance merely through escalation.

## Control Expectations

Controls must be appropriate to the consequence of failure. Material controls should be measurable, testable, traceable, and supported by retrievable evidence.

## Reporting

Risk reporting should answer:

- What changed?
- Why does it matter?
- What action is being taken?
- What decision or escalation is required?

## Review

This policy shall be reviewed periodically and whenever the organization's scale, regulatory status, control authority, or systemic consequence materially changes.
