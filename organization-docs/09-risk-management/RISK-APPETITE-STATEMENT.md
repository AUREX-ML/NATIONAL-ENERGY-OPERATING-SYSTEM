# Risk Appetite Statement

## Purpose

Risk appetite defines the amount and type of risk the organization is willing to pursue or retain while executing its mission.

Risk appetite enables innovation by making boundaries explicit.

## General Position

The organization accepts that meaningful infrastructure innovation requires technical, commercial, strategic, and execution risk. It therefore maintains a higher appetite for controlled experimentation and learning than for risks capable of causing human harm, unlawful operation, fraud, systemic infrastructure damage, or irreversible loss of trust.

## Appetite by Domain

| Domain | General Appetite | Position |
|---|---|---|
| Research & experimentation | High within bounded environments | Encourage rapid learning with isolation and reversibility |
| Product innovation | Moderate–High | Accept uncertainty through staged validation |
| Technology architecture | Moderate | Experiment before production; protect interoperability and recoverability |
| Commercial / market | Moderate | Accept market uncertainty without uncontrolled contractual exposure |
| Financial | Moderate–Low | Protect liquidity and organizational continuity |
| Operational reliability | Low–Moderate | Controlled degradation may be acceptable; systemic unmanaged failure is not |
| Cybersecurity | Low | Very low tolerance for unauthorized infrastructure control or critical compromise |
| Data protection | Low | Minimize unnecessary collection and unauthorized disclosure |
| Regulatory / legal | Low | No deliberate operation outside applicable authority |
| Safety | Very Low | Human safety is a hard boundary |
| Fraud / bribery / corruption | Near Zero | Prohibited conduct is not accepted for commercial advantage |
| Reputation | Low–Moderate | Accept reasoned innovation controversy; avoid preventable trust failures |

## Tolerance

Risk tolerance translates appetite into measurable operating boundaries.

Where possible, each material risk should define:

- normal operating range;
- warning threshold;
- tolerance breach;
- hard limit where applicable;
- required action and escalation.

```text
GREEN  → Monitor
AMBER  → Investigate / Treat
RED    → Escalate / Remediate
HARD LIMIT → Contain / Stop where required
```

## Hard Boundaries

The following principles are not traded against revenue, growth, investor pressure, customer pressure, or schedule:

- verified human safety constraints;
- deliberate bribery, fraud, or corruption;
- knowingly unlawful infrastructure operation;
- knowingly unauthorized critical control access;
- deliberate concealment of material incidents or evidence;
- intentional circumvention of mandatory safety interlocks without authorized engineering justification and controlled procedure.

## Scaling Principle

Risk appetite may remain strategically ambitious while operational tolerance becomes stricter as control authority and systemic consequence increase.

```text
Laboratory → Pilot → Commercial EMS → Regional DERMS → National DERMS
        Increasing consequence → Increasing control rigor
```

## Approval and Review

Risk appetite is a governance instrument. Material changes require appropriate executive and, at institutional maturity, governing-body approval.
