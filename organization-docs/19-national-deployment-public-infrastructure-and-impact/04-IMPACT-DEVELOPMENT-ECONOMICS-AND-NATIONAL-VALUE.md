# Session 4 — Impact, Development Economics & National Value

## Purpose

Define how national DERMS infrastructure connects engineering outputs to measurable energy, economic, institutional and public outcomes.

## Impact Chain

```text
INPUTS
  ↓
ACTIVITIES
  ↓
OUTPUTS
  ↓
OUTCOMES
  ↓
IMPACT
```

Installed devices and connected sites are outputs. They are not automatically impact.

## Value Dimensions

National value may include:

- Improved DER visibility
- Faster fault detection and maintenance
- Improved asset utilization
- Reduced downtime
- Reduced energy losses or avoidable waste
- Better renewable integration
- Improved flexibility
- Improved reliability and resilience
- Better infrastructure records
- Revenue assurance
- Reduced lifecycle cost
- Local technical capability
- Job creation
- Better investment decisions
- Stronger public infrastructure stewardship

## Measurement Architecture

Every material impact claim should identify:

- Indicator
- Definition
- Baseline
- Data source
- Method
- Frequency
- Owner
- Target where applicable
- Limitations
- Verification status

## Counterfactual Discipline

Where claiming impact, distinguish what happened from what can reasonably be attributed to the intervention. Correlation should not be presented as causation without supporting methodology.

## Economic Analysis

Relevant economic measures may include:

- Capital expenditure
- Operating expenditure
- Total cost of ownership
- Avoided cost
- Energy savings
- Revenue improvement
- Reliability value
- Maintenance savings
- Payback period
- NPV/IRR where appropriate
- Cost per site/resource served
- Cost per verified outcome

## Public Value

Public infrastructure should be evaluated beyond direct financial return. Public value may include reliability, resilience, transparency, institutional capability, equitable service improvement and long-term infrastructure sustainability.

## Evidence Status

Claims should distinguish:

- Measured result
- Estimate
- Modelled result
- Forecast
- Target

## Impact Register

Impact evidence should trace from physical reality through telemetry, data and indicators to the eventual claim. Material claims should remain reproducible and auditable.

## Learning Loop

```text
DEPLOYMENT → OPERATION → MEASUREMENT → IMPACT EVIDENCE → DECISION → IMPROVEMENT
```

Impact evidence therefore influences where to scale, redesign, pause or stop investment.
