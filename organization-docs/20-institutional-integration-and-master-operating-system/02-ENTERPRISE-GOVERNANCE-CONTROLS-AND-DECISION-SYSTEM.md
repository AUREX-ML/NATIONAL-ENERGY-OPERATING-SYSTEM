# Session 2 — Enterprise Governance, Controls & Decision System

## Purpose

Establish the enterprise-level authority, decision, risk, control and escalation architecture binding all organizational domains.

## Governance Hierarchy

```text
Purpose
  ↓
Governance
  ↓
Policy
  ↓
Standard
  ↓
Procedure / Process
  ↓
Execution
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Assurance
```

## Decision Architecture

Every material institutional decision should identify:

- Decision class
- Competent authority
- Accountable owner
- Required consultation
- Evidence required
- Decision record
- Escalation threshold

Decision authority should remain as close to competent execution as consequence permits.

Routine decisions should not unnecessarily climb the hierarchy.

## Escalation Architecture

```text
Issue
 ↓
Local resolution?
 ├─ Yes → Resolve and record where required
 └─ No  → Domain escalation
              ↓
        Cross-domain escalation
              ↓
       Executive / Governance
```

Escalation exists for exceptions, exceeded authority, unresolved conflict and unacceptable consequence—not for routine decision-making.

## Enterprise Risk Integration

Domain risks feed the enterprise risk system while preserving systemic relationships. Risk aggregation must examine combinations such as funding + supply chain + deployment, or cybersecurity + remote control + public infrastructure.

## Control Architecture

Controls should be reusable across domains where possible. Each control should trace to the risks, obligations or requirements it addresses and to the evidence demonstrating operation.

## Exception Governance

Critical exceptions must remain visible, including:

- Expired exceptions
- Critical control failures
- Overdue findings
- Unaccepted risks
- Blocked readiness gates

## Governance Cadence

A proportionate institutional rhythm may include:

- Continuous operational monitoring
- Weekly execution review
- Monthly portfolio, finance and risk review
- Quarterly strategy, maturity and architecture review
- Annual mission, governance and institutional architecture review

Meetings exist to produce decisions, accountability and corrective action—not merely reporting activity.

## Definition of Done

Enterprise governance is integrated when authority, decision routing, escalation, enterprise risk, reusable controls, exceptions and governance cadence operate under a coherent institutional model.
