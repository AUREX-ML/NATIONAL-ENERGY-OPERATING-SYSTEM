# Organization Growth Framework

This section defines how the organization grows from Facility Energy Management into regional, national, and eventually ecosystem-scale digital energy infrastructure.

## Documents

- [Organization Growth Framework](./organization-growth-framework.md)

## Purpose

The Organization Vision defines the destination.
The Founder's Thesis explains why the organization should exist.
The Organization Growth Framework defines how the organization earns the right to take on progressively larger coordination responsibilities.

The framework treats growth as an increase in trusted responsibility, stakeholder collaboration, ecosystem participation, and service area—not merely headcount, funding, or software scope.
