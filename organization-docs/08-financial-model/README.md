# 08 — Financial Model

The **Financial Model** defines how the organization creates, captures, finances, protects, measures, governs, and reinvests economic value as it develops energy-management infrastructure from the facility level toward Regional and National DERMS capability.

> **Financial thesis:** The organization converts capital into reusable coordination capability, converts that capability into measurable customer and infrastructure value, earns sustainable revenue by accepting defined operational responsibility, protects the resulting cash through disciplined financial governance, and reinvests surplus to expand the amount of energy infrastructure it can responsibly coordinate.

## North Star Relationship

The organizational North Star is **Energy Infrastructure Under Trusted Coordination**.

The Financial Model answers whether the organization has the economic capacity to sustain and expand that responsibility.

Mission progress and financial health are therefore measured together, but they are not confused.

## Master Financial Law

> **Every financial decision should strengthen, or deliberately and measurably advance, the organization's ability to coordinate energy infrastructure responsibly over the long term.**
