# Governance & Assurance

This section defines the authority, risk, assurance, and evidence discipline needed for an energy coordination platform.

## Core Governance Position

Technical capability does not create legal, regulatory, commercial, or operational authority.

Every monitoring, coordination, dispatch, aggregation, settlement, or market function must operate within explicit authority granted by law, regulation, grid code, contract, delegation, consent, or approved operating procedure.

## Assurance Chain

```text
Requirement
  ↓
Control
  ↓
Execution
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Assurance
  ↓
Readiness decision
```

## Government Review Questions

- Which public authority has mandate over each function?
- Which activities require regulatory approval or licensing?
- What grid-code and utility requirements apply?
- What cybersecurity controls are mandatory before field connection?
- What data-protection and consent requirements apply?
- What evidence is required before pilot, procurement, or national scale-up?
