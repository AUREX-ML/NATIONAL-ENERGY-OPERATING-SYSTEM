# Contributing

## Purpose

This repository contains working programme architecture for the National Energy Operating System Programme. Contributions should improve clarity, evidence, technical correctness, institutional usefulness, or implementation readiness.

## Current Stage

The programme is at concept, architecture, and validation-preparation stage. Contributions should not present the programme as approved, deployed, procured, or operational unless that status is supported by explicit evidence and authority.

## Contribution Areas

Useful contributions include:

- corrections to technical architecture;
- regulatory, legal, or institutional clarifications;
- source-backed sector evidence;
- pilot-readiness improvements;
- cybersecurity, data-governance, or assurance improvements;
- implementation roadmap improvements;
- documentation cleanup and navigation improvements;
- issue reports identifying gaps, duplication, unclear claims, or unsupported assumptions.

## Evidence Standard

Material claims should be supported by one of:

- official policy, legal, regulatory, or institutional source;
- utility, system-operator, regulator, or government publication;
- field data or pilot evidence;
- peer-reviewed or credible technical source;
- clearly identified expert review;
- documented engineering assumption marked for validation.

Unsupported claims should be rewritten as hypotheses or removed.

## Authority and Safety

Do not submit changes that imply:

- regulatory approval has been granted when it has not;
- the programme has authority over assets or institutions without explicit authorization;
- remote control is permitted without safety, legal, contractual, and operational controls;
- national deployment is ready before facility and regional evidence exists.

## Pull Request Expectations

Pull requests should:

- describe the problem being solved;
- explain what changed;
- identify affected documents;
- state whether claims are supported by evidence or require validation;
- preserve clear distinction between target architecture and current reality.

## Style

Use clear institutional language. Prefer direct statements, defined status, and reviewable evidence over promotional wording.
